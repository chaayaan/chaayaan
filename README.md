## FineBullion Desk — Precious Metal Dealer Management Platform

**Management platform for gold dealers**

FineBullion Desk is a business management platform I built for precious metal (gold) dealers, handling gold exchange, purchases, and multi-karat sales where dealers convert **old or impure gold — brought in by customers such as artisans — into pure gold**, while maintaining accurate inventory and vault accounting. The system synchronizes inventory automatically across exchanges, purchases, and multi-karat gold sales.

### Core Modules

- Gold Exchange Management
- Multi-Karat Gold Sales
- Gold Purchase Management
- Live Inventory Management
- 24K Vault Tracking
- Expense Management
- Dashboard & Reports
- Customer Records (including artisan and individual sellers)

### Business Logic

- Convert impure or old gold into pure gold
- Manage 18K, 20K, 21K, 22K, and 24K inventory separately
- Automatic inventory deduction during exchanges and sales
- Real-time 24K vault balance tracking
- Weight-based accounting for customer gold transactions

**Tech Stack:** `PHP` • `MySQL` • `JavaScript` • `AJAX` • `Bootstrap`
